## Demonstrate parallel coordinates

1. Open the page and confirm 175 countries in the default matched 2020 view.
2. Keep matched membership and fixed axes checked. Compare bundling 0% and 35%; individual axis intercepts stay fixed.
3. Drag vertically on an axis. The brush highlights a range and updates the matching-country count. Multiple brushes combine across axes. Use Clear brushes to reset.
4. Click an axis title; it moves one position right. Keyboard Enter also reorders an axis with focus. This action clears brushes.
5. Switch year, region and scale controls and inspect the status. Hover a line for country measurements.

The bundle curves move toward adoption-band centroid midpoints. The midpoint is constrained between its two measured endpoint values to avoid artificial humps. This is not a claim to reproduce every detail of a published bundling algorithm. Values at the axes remain the observations; intermediate curves are visual interpolation.

## Demonstrate the treemap

1. Start at the root with Region -> country selected. The 184 leaves sum to 4,521,767,530 included users.
2. Click Asia or another group to zoom. Inspect breadcrumb, group totals, and click 'Back one level' to zoom out.
3. Hover a country: area encodes user count; color encodes adoption percentage. Group tiles are neutral.
4. Switch to Adoption band -> region -> country. The root total and leaves remain unchanged. Explore a band, then region, and return.

Different zoom subsets have different displayed totals. That is not a change in underlying country values. Root-level comparisons are appropriate for checking equal leaf universes.
